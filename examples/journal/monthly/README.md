## Placement

Use `period-review@v1` records under the calendar-year directory. Inspect the
month's daily evidence as well as weekly reviews. Weeks can cross month
boundaries; select events by their local date instead of copying whole weeks.

Refresh materially affected closed months when corrections arrive. Preserve
historical open questions at the period end and identify any later evidence.
