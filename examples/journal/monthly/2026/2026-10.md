## Lessons

Assessment: the reusable lesson is to preserve the boundaries between an event,
its evidence, a decision, and a delivered change. The repeated pressure to
expand was less important than the evidence needed to justify doing so.
The unconfirmed locale hypothesis, corrected event count, and distinction between
a fixture test and production correction illustrate that judgment.
