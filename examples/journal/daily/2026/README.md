## Placement

Use `journal-entry@v1` records named YYYY-MM-DD.md, with a matching date field.
Times are local to Europe/Madrid. Preserve original blocks and append a dated
correction or late-evidence block when needed.

## Navigation

Look up a date directly or search the records by subject or source identifier.
The catalog deliberately omits individual daily records. Coverage is reported
inside period reviews, not inferred from this map or from an absent filename.
